# Tool Shed — raw idea notes

People in our apartment block buy power drills, pressure washers and ladders, use them twice a year, and leave them in a cupboard. A neighbour ends up buying the exact same drill a week later.

Idea: a simple neighbourhood tool-lending app where residents list tools they own and neighbours borrow them for a weekend.

Validation so far:
- I pitched this to five friends over coffee and all five said they love the idea and would definitely use it.
- A competitor website claims households save 40% on home tools by sharing.

Money / effort: haven't worked out pricing or a deposit yet. Not sure how long we should test this before giving up. No tech stack chosen yet — just want to get the idea down cleanly first.
