# 1. Context

## 1. The idea
A booking portal for a 6-chair dental clinic where patients pick an open slot and receptionists confirm or reschedule visits without back-and-forth phone calls.

## 2. The problem
- **Who hurts:** patients waiting on hold and two receptionists handling 70 booking calls a day.
- **What happens today:** every booking and reschedule is taken by phone and typed into a shared spreadsheet; 14% of calls go unanswered during morning peak.
- **Evidence:** clinic phone log from October 2026 (1,420 calls, 199 missed) and 3 weeks of receptionist tally sheets (average 3.5 minutes per call).

## 3. Goals and stop condition
- **Target:** at least 40% of appointments booked online within 60 days of launch; missed morning calls below 5%.
- **Stop condition:** if online bookings stay below 15% after 60 days, pause new features and audit patient onboarding.

## 4. Boundaries
- **In scope (Stage 1):** slot search, online booking request, receptionist confirmation, cancellation.
- **Out of scope:** insurance claims, treatment plans, online prepayments.

## 5. Money and effort
- **Budget:** 2 engineers for 5 weeks.
- **Expected return:** saves ~2.5 hours of receptionist phone time per day.
